- **Industry:** AI/Voice Technology SaaS

## Design Style Guidelines

**Overall Aesthetic:**
- Modern and minimalist
- Clean lines and geometric shapes
- Subtle gradients (indigo to purple primary)
- Professional but approachable
- Tech-forward without being too futuristic
- Works well in both light and dark themes

**Typography (For Logo):**
- Modern sans-serif font
- Bold, readable
- Tech-friendly aesthetic
- Consider fonts like: Inter, Poppins, Space Grotesk, or custom

**Avoid:**
- Overly complex designs
- Too many colors
- Cluttered elements
- Hard-to-read text at small sizes
- Cliché tech imagery (circuits, chips overused)

**Prefer:**
- Simple, memorable symbols
- Scalable designs
- Versatile color schemes
- Unique identity
- Voice/sound wave elements
- Subtle French connection (not too obvious)
